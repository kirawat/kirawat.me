## Styles

### APA (American Psychological Association)

Predominantly used in the social sciences (e.g., psychology, education, sociology, communication) and some natural sciences. It emphasizes the date of publication because timeline is often crucial in these fields.

APA style lists the author's last name first, followed by their initials, is to facilitate easy and accurate alphabetization of the reference list. When readers are looking for a specific source mentioned in your text, they can quickly scan an alphabetized reference list by the author's last name. This is much more efficient than trying to find a first name in a long list. Last names are also generally more unique within a list than first names. While not foolproof (multiple authors can have the same last name), it's a more reliable primary sorter.

#### Format

##### Website

Author Last name, First name. (Year, Month Day). "Title of work". *Website Name*. Retrieved on Month Day, Year. URL

##### Social Media

Author Last name, First name \[@username\]. (Year, Month Day). *Content of the post up to the first 20 words* \[Description of audiovisuals if any\]\[Post\]. *Name of Platform*. Retrieved on Month Day, Year. URL

### Chicago Manual of Style

Widely used in the humanities (e.g., history, literature, arts). It offers two systems:

* **Notes and Bibliography:** Uses footnotes or endnotes for citations and a bibliography at the end. Thsi is very common in history and allows for more discursive notes.

* **Author-Date:** Similar to [APA](#apa-american-psychological-association), using in-text parenthetical citations and a reference list. This is often used in social sciences and some natural sciences.

#### Format

##### Website

Author First Name Last Name, "Title of Article," *Website Name*, Publication Date (or last modified date), Accessed Month Day, Year. URL.

##### Social Media

Author First Name Last Name (@username), "Full text of post (or first 160 characters)," *Name of Platform*, Month Day, Year, \[Time if available\], Accessed Month Day, Year, URL.
