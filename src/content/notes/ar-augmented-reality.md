name: Augmented Reality (AR)
dateCreated: 2025-06-14 17:21:00 +0700
---
AR, or Augmented Reality, is a specific technology that overlays computer-generated images and information onto the user's view of the real world. This is typically achieved through devices like smartphones, tablets, or specialized glasses. The key characteristic of AR is that it enhances or "augments" the user's existing environment, rather than replacing it. Popular examples include mobile games like "Pokémon Go", where digital creatures appear in the real world through a phone's camera, or applications that allow you to virtually place furniture in your room before you buy it.
