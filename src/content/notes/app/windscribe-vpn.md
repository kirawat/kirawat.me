Windscribe is a Canadian-based Virtual Private Network (VPN) provider, offers a combination of a desktop VPN application and a browser extension designed to block ads, trackers, and provide secure internet access.

## Controversies

### Jurisdiction in a Five Eyes Country

Windscribe is headquartered in Canada, a member of the Five Eyes intelligence-sharing alliance (along with the US, UK, Australia, and New Zealand). This raises privacy concerns for some users, as member countries have been known to share intelligence and surveillance data. While Windscribe maintains a no-logging policy, its location remains a point of contention for privacy purists.

### The 2025 Greek Court Case

Windscribe's co-founder, Yegor Sak, was personally charged by Greek authorities in connection with alleged illegal activities conducted by a Windscribe user. The authorities traced an IP address used for sending spam emails back to a Windscribe server. However, because Windscribe adheres to its no-logging policy, it could not provide any information to identify the user. The case was ultimately dismissed, which in a way, served as a real-world validation of their no-logging claims. Nevertheless, the incident brought a significant amount of legal and public scrutiny to the company.

### Professionalism

There have been occasional criticisms regarding the company's informal and sometimes blunt communication style on public forums.
