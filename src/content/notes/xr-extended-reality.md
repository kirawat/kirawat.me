name: Extended Reality (XR)
dateCreated: 2025-06-14 17:18:00 +0700
---
XR, or Extended Reality, is a universal descriptor for all technologies that merge the real and virtual worlds, or create entirely immersive experiences. It is the superset that includes [Augmented Reality (AR)](ar-augmented-reality.md), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR).
