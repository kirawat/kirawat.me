## Citation

With [Chicago Notes and Bibliography style](citation.md#chicago-manual-of-style), you can include more of the source's content or your own annotations directly within the footnote that constitutes your citation. This make the note itself more self-contained and immediately informative.

However, if simplicity and a clear separation between a brief in-text cue and a full reference list are more appealing, [APA](citation.md#apa-american-psychological-association) is a good choice.
