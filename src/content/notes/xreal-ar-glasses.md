### XREAL One

XREAL One is a pair of augmented reality (AR) glasses designed to function as a wearable personal display. Released as part of the "One Series," these glasses represent an advancement in the consumer AR space, primarily by integrating a proprietary chip that enhances their spatial computing capabilities.

The self-developed X1 chip allows the glasses to perform 3DoF (three degrees of freedom) tracking natively, without needing to be tethered to an external processing unit like XREAL's Beam Pro for spatial functionality. This means users can "anchor" the virtual screen in a fixed position in space, and it will stay there even when they turn their head.

The XREAL One supports several viewing modes to suit different use cases:

  * **Anchor Mode (3DoF):** Pins the screen in a fixed location in the user's environment.

  * **Follow Mode (0DoF):** The screen stays locked in the center of the user's vision, gently following their gaze.

  * **Side View:** Shrinks the screen and pins it to the side, allowing for multitasking while maintaining a clear view of the real world.

  * **Ultrawide Mode:** Creates an expansive, curved virtual display ideal for productivity and an immersive cinematic experience.

The glasses are equipped with Sony Micro-OLED displays, offering a crisp 1080p resolution for each eye and a high refresh rate of up to 120Hz. The lenses also feature adjustable electrochromic dimming, allowing users to control the transparency and visibility of the real world.

In a notable collaboration, the audio system in the XREAL One is tuned by Bose, delivering a high-fidelity sound experience directly from the glasses' temples.

At its core, the XREAL One is engineered to provide users with a large, private virtual screen for a variety of applications, including entertainment, gaming, and productivity. It connects via a USB-C cable to a wide range of devices such as smartphones, laptops, PCs, and handheld gaming consoles like the Steam Deck.
