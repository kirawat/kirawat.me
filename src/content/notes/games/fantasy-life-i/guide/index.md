## Crafting

### Quality

Quality is based on how many turns are left when you finished crafting.
  * **Good Quality:** you need 3+ turns.
  * **Great Quality:** you need 5+ turns.
  * **Top Quality:** you need 7+ turns.

### Rank

Rank is only available on weapons and tools. There are 5 ranks in total: **"Ordinary"**, **"Fine"**, **"Notable"**, **"Supreme"**, and **"Legendary"**.

Most tools and weapons have 3 options, unlocked as you progress the crafter's Life.
