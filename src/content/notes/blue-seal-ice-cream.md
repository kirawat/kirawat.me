## History

Blue Seal was originally established in Okinawa in 1948 at a U.S. military base by the American company Foremost Dairies. It was initially set up to provide dairy products to Americans stationed there after World War II.

In 1963, the company expanded its operations and began selling ice cream to the Okinawan public. Its later became an independent Okinawa company in 1976. However, it retained the well-established name and logo due to its strong brand recognition and heritage.

## Branding

The name "Blue Seal" was inspired by the "Blue Ribbon Award", a prestigious recognition given to high-quality dairy products in the United States.

As a subsidiary or operation of Foremost Dairies, Blue Seal naturally adopted branding elements similar to its parent company, including the shield shape and color scheme often associated with Foremost.
